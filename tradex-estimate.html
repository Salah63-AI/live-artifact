Project cost estimate

Build the cost of one indoor unit from the price list, set the number of units and the project's location zone, then add overhead, indirect cost and marketing and price it for the margin. Direct = (material + subcontractor) × zone %. Total cost = direct + 15% + 25% + marketing. Sales price = total cost ÷ 0.8, less discount, plus 14% VAT.
